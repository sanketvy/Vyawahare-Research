# 04. Financial Engineering & Indian Markets Notes

Domain principles, valuation mathematics, and forensic accounting guidelines:
- **Indian Market Structure:** BSE/NSE market mechanics, SEBI disclosure regulations, MCA filings.
- **Valuation Models:** Discounted Cash Flow (DCF), Dividend Discount Model (DDM), peer EV/EBITDA and P/E multiples.
- **Forensic Checks:** Beneish M-Score, Altman Z-Score, related-party transaction auditing, cash flow vs net profit divergence.
