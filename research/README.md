# Research

Domain research, financial engineering principles, and Indian equity market methodology.

### Scope & Modules:
- **Indian Market Rules & Filings:** BSE/NSE market mechanics, SEBI compliance, MCA corporate filings, quarterly results, concalls.
- **Fundamental & Forensic Analysis:** Valuation models (DCF, peer multiples), DuPont analysis, debt-equity profiling, Altman Z-score, forensic auditor checks.
- **Report Templates:** Institutional-grade report standards, SWOT structures, and management commentary extraction guidelines.
